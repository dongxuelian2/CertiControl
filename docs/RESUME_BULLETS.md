## Portfolio emphasis variants

### Mathematics / research engineering

CertiControl turns classical control-theory criteria into inspectable computational certificates: algebraic evidence, witnesses, residuals, margins, and structural transformations are retained alongside the final verdict.

### Software engineering

CertiControl separates a deterministic mathematical core from orchestration, reporting, and Streamlit presentation layers, with partial-analysis isolation, JSON-safe nested certificates, reproducible examples, and cross-version CI.

### Demo / hackathon

CertiControl shows the evidence behind a control result: why a model is uncontrollable, whether a stability claim is tolerance-sensitive, whether an LQR solution actually stabilizes the closed loop, and how controllability/observability split across the state space.
